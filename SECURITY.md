# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **tee@oaksphere.co** with what you found and how to reproduce it. You will get an
acknowledgement within two business days and either a fix or a dated plan within ten.

Please do not open a public issue for a security report.

## Scope

OakSphere builds and runs automation inside infrastructure our clients own. If the issue
affects one client's deployment rather than this organization's own code, say so in the
email so it routes to the right place.
