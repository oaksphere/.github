### Your company, AI-ready.

OakSphere gets businesses ready for the AI era. It consolidates a company's knowledge, SOPs,
and data into a central brain, deploys AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT connected to
that brain and to the company's existing tools, and builds monitored automations across
platforms like GoHighLevel, n8n, Zapier, Notion, and Airtable — plus a mesh of AI agents
that watch, analyze, and act around the clock without waiting for a prompt. Everything is
built in accounts the client owns and verified with live coverage and accuracy reporting —
clients keep their platforms, their data, and their independence.

#### What we build

| | |
|---|---|
| **The Brain** | Your knowledge, SOPs, and data consolidated into one structured, queryable layer you own |
| **The Interface** | Claude or ChatGPT on top, wired into your real tools, so your team commands the business in plain language |
| **The Hands** | Automations across the stack you already run — built, monitored, and verified |
| **The Mesh** | A coordinated fleet of AI agents on autopilot — watching, analyzing, and acting around the clock |
